The north star is **$1,000,000 USD in profit for Daniel**. Every decision, code change, recommendation, and delegation should be evaluated against: "Does this move us closer to $1M profit?"

**Why:** Daniel explicitly defined this as the mission. It's not just about building websites — it's about building a profitable business machine.

**How to apply:**
- Prioritize work that generates revenue or reduces costs
- Identify automation opportunities that scale without proportional cost increase
- When offering options, frame them in terms of business impact, not just technical merit
- Track and document what's working and what's not in the Daily vault
- Look for leverage points: templates, automation, repeatable processes
