4. Email & Lead Generation Sources

4.1 MedillaForms — Medical Bill Leads

Direct Consumer Email Lists — LIMITED but available

There is no "people with medical debt" email list sold directly. However, proxy targeting works:

  • Experian Mailing Lists — Consumer data with 100+ demographic filters. Target: income brackets, ZIP codes with high uninsured rates, age groups, credit score ranges. experian.com/small-business/mailing-lists
  • Data Axle USA — B2C lists with deep demographic segmentation. Can target by: likely uninsured, income <$40K, age 25-55, Hispanic ethnicity, geographic (CA, TX, FL, AZ). Custom quotes.
  • LeadsPlease — Consumer mailing lists starting at $299.75 for 1,000 records. Demographic filters available.
  • BookYourData — Pay-as-you-go, $99 for 250 contacts. Credit-based, B2C available.

Recommended: Facebook/Instagram Ads to Build Email List

Instead of buying lists (high risk, low quality for this niche), build your own through lead gen ads:

  • Facebook Lead Ads — Target: Spanish speakers, ages 25-55, in CA/TX/FL/AZ/NY, interests: "medical bills," "hospital," "health insurance," "Medicaid," "financial hardship"
  • Estimated CPL: $5-15 per lead in Spanish (much lower than English due to less competition)
  • Lead Magnet: "Calcula cuánto puedes ahorrar en tu factura médica en 2 minutos" — free assessment, captures email
  • Google Ads: Keywords: "reducir factura médica," "apelar factura hospital," "charity care español," "ayuda facturas médicas," "error en factura médica." Est. CPC: $0.80-$2.00 in Spanish

4.2 MedillaDisability — SSDI/SSI Leads

Commercial SSDI Lead Providers — ABUNDANT

SSDI leads are a well-established category in legal lead generation. Multiple vendors sell real-time, exclusive SSDI/SSI leads:

  • Legal Brand Marketing — 100% exclusive, real-time SSDI and SSI leads from individuals "actively seeking representation." legalbrandmarketing.com/exclusive-social-security-disability-leads/
  • The Leads Warehouse — SSDI leads with quick delivery via API or email. Multi-level compliance. Proven for telemarketing, texting, emailing. theleadswarehouse.com/social-security-disability-insurance/
  • On Point Legal Leads — Premium quality SSDI leads for lawyers. onpointlegalleads.com/social-security-disability-leads/
  • Best Case Leads — SSD leads sent via email in real-time or posted directly to CRM. Includes: name, phone, email, city. bestcaseleads.com/leads/ssdi-or-social-security-disability/
  • LeadOrder — Disability leads & SSDI benefit leads. Services include lead gen, mailing lists, SEM, consulting. leadorder.com/leads/disability-leads
  • Concussion Media — Efficient generation of SSDI leads at scale. concussionmedia.com/social-security-disability-leads/
  • Assure Disability — Lead generation and lead sales for disability advocates. myassureservices.com/leads/
Important: Most SSDI lead providers sell to law firms. We'd need to position as a "disability application assistance service" (not legal representation). The leads typically cost $15-45 per lead depending on exclusivity and filters. This is commercially viable because our revenue per customer ($79-99) gives us 2-6x ROAS on lead spend alone.

Organic + Paid Strategy for SSDI Leads

  • Facebook Ads CPL for Legal: $72.40 avg (Focus Digital July 2025). But Spanish-language targeting significantly reduces this — estimate $15-30 CPL in Spanish with less competition.
  • Google Ads: "aplicar para disability," "abogado de disability gratis," "SSDI español," "seguro social por incapacidad," "denegación de disability apelación." These are high-intent, lower-competition Spanish keywords.
  • Facebook Groups: Latino community groups, disability support groups, "Viviendo con Discapacidad," chronic illness communities. Organic engagement drives trust.
  • YouTube: "Cómo llenar la aplicación de SSDI paso a paso," "Por qué niegan disability," "Cómo apelar una denegación de SSDI." These are evergreen content that generates leads for years.

10. Risk Assessment & Legal Compliance

RiskSeverityMedillaForms MitigationMedillaDisability Mitigation
UPL (Unauthorized Practice of Law) HIGH Every interaction: "No somos abogados. Esto es una guía educativa. No damos asesoría legal." Never say "legal advice." Never call ourselves "representatives." We are an "application preparation assistant." Like TurboTax for disability. Disclaimers in Spanish on every screen.
SSA Compliance HIGH N/A — medical bills don't involve SSA Must not charge for SSA services. We charge for the GUIDE/ASSISTANCE, not the application itself. Same model as lawyers who charge for "preparation." $79 flat fee, not contingent on approval.
AI Accuracy MEDIUM Medical bills have standard codes. AI is good at pattern recognition. Still: human review for flagged cases. "Si encontramos errores" messaging sets correct expectations. SSDI applications are complex. AI can help but must be tested rigorously. Start with structured data collection, not full auto-fill. Human QA on every application before submission.
Data Privacy MEDIUM Medical bills contain PHI. Must be HIPAA-aware (not necessarily HIPAA-covered as a consumer tool, but must handle data carefully). Encrypt at rest. No storing bills after analysis complete. SSDI applications contain SSN, medical history, work history. Critical data. SOC2-compliant infrastructure. WhatsApp encryption. Data retention policy.
