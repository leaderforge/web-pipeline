What We Do Heavy-Duty Fabrication & Repair

Custom Dump Bodies

End dump, super dump, and transfer bodies built to your truck and your load. Steel or aluminum — your choice, our precision.

Chassis Extension

Frame stretching and reinforcement for longer beds and heavier payloads. Engineered to handle the load — day in, day out.

Suspension Systems

Heavy-duty suspension upgrades for dump applications. Leaf spring, air ride, and custom setups that keep your truck stable under load.

Repair & Restoration

Full restoration of worn dump bodies. Hydraulic repair, floor replacement, structural welding — your truck back on the road, stronger.
