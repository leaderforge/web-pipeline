Commercial Roofing

TPO & PVC Systems

Energy-efficient single-ply membranes built for California's climate. UV-reflective, weather-resistant, backed by warranties up to 30 years.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and corrugated metal systems with 50+ year lifespan. Steel and aluminum resist high winds, heat, and hail with minimal upkeep.

Modified Bitumen

Multi-layer torch-down and self-adhered systems for flat and low-slope commercial roofs. Combines asphalt waterproofing with polymer reinforcement.

Coatings & Waterproofing

Silicone and acrylic coatings that extend your roof's life 10-15 years. Seamless waterproof membrane stops leaks and reflects up to 85% of UV rays.

24/7 Emergency Repair

Fast, reliable emergency repair — day or night. Two-hour commercial response. One call dispatches a certified technician to your location.

Inspections & Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance that catches small issues before they become expensive repairs. Each visit includes inspection, photo documentation, and a detailed report.

Residential Roofing

Full Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and replacement with GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed materials. Manufacturer-spec installation with warranty registration included.

Roof Leak Repair

Same-day or next-day inspection. We identify root causes and present a transparent written repair plan. Temporary protection provided if needed.

Gutter & Downspout Repair

Seamless gutter installation, guard systems, and downspout service. Prevents water damage, fascia rot, and attic moisture.

Skylight Installation & Repair

New skylight installations, replacements, and leak repairs with proper flashing and weathertight warranty. Solar tubes available.

Roof Insulation

Attic insulation analysis and upgrades that may qualify for rebates. Reduces heat transfer, lowers energy bills, and prevents moisture buildup.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, straightforward answers to common questions about our roofing services.

Homeowners can book a full range of residential services including: roof inspection and condition assessment (checking for missing shingles, flashing issues, water intrusion, granule loss); localized repairs (patching leaks, replacing damaged shingles or tiles, repairing flashing, vent boots, gutters, downspouts); partial re-roofing or roof deck repair; full roof replacement; and roof maintenance plans (annual cleaning, debris removal, gutter clearing, ventilation checks).
TRS Roofing Co. provides emergency roof repair throughout our California service area for sudden issues like storm-damage leaks, falling tree branches, missing shingles after high winds, or water intrusion during heavy rain. We're available 24/7 for emergency response. When you call, we dispatch a certified technician quickly, secure the site, provide a written estimate, handle safe cleanup, and document the issue for insurance claims.
TRS Roofing Co. offers a free, no-obligation roof inspection as part of our commitment to transparency. During the inspection we: assess the roof's current condition, note visible damage, identify potential leak points, check flashing and ventilation, and document findings with photos. You'll then receive a detailed, no-pressure quote for any necessary repair or replacement work.
TRS Roofing Co. has extensive experience working with homeowners' insurance claims for roof damage (hail, wind, fallen debris, fire-damaged roofing). We assist by: documenting damage with photos and detailed reports; supporting claim preparation and coordinating with adjusters if needed; advising on building code upgrades and material replacements insurers may require; and providing the invoice, warranty information, and project scope for the claim file.
TRS Roofing Co. offers rapid-response roof-leak repair across our California service area. Upon contact we: schedule same-day or next-day inspection depending on urgency; provide temporary fixes (tarpaulins, emergency flashing, water removal) if necessary; identify root causes (shingle damage, flashing failure, vent boot leak, gutter blockage); and present a written repair plan and cost estimate. We recommend contacting us at the first sign of a leak.
